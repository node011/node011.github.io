operator@c2

Red team operator focused on full-chain adversary emulation — initial access through domain and cloud dominance, with an emphasis on staying quiet while doing it.

I build and run offensive infrastructure end to end: phishing and payload chains, redirector meshes, custom C2 profiles, and loaders written to survive modern EDR telemetry rather than merely bypass a signature. On the research side I spend my time on Kubernetes and cloud identity attack paths — token abuse, over-permissive service accounts, container escapes, and the trust relationships nobody diagrams.

Most of my work is assumed-breach and purple-team engagements, which means the deliverable is never just a finding list. Every operation ends with detection gaps mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, working detection logic handed to the blue team, and a replayable timeline so defenders can prove the fix.

I write my own tooling when the public option is burned, and I publish what's safe to publish. Findings get reported, disclosed responsibly, and — where the vendor cooperates — turned into advisories rather than conference slides.
